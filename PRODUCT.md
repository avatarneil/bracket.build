# Product

## Register

product

## Users

NFL fans checking the current schedule, following live games, reviewing final scores, and building postseason predictions on phones and desktops.

## Product Purpose

bracket.build provides one season-aware place to follow NFL games and, when the postseason begins, create and share playoff predictions. Success means the app opens to the part of the season that matters now while keeping upcoming phases easy to reach.

## Brand Personality

Direct, energetic, and trustworthy. The experience should feel familiar to sports fans, make game state immediately legible, and keep the interface focused on the games.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic sports-betting aesthetics, decorative dashboard chrome, dense broadcast-style clutter, and interactions that hide ordinary navigation behind custom controls. Team colors should communicate identity without overwhelming scores or status.

## Design Principles

- Open on what matters now, while keeping the rest of the season one clear action away.
- Make live, upcoming, and final states distinguishable without relying on color alone.
- Put teams, time, and score ahead of secondary detail.
- Preserve familiar sports conventions and predictable navigation.
- Keep the core experience fast and usable on a phone during a game.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA, support full keyboard navigation and visible focus, provide semantic status labels, preserve browser zoom, honor reduced-motion preferences, and use redundant cues for live and final states. Interactive targets must be at least 24px on desktop and 44px on mobile.
